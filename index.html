What it brings together

One system. Essential workflows.

A modular academic project exploring how software can organize common hospital administration tasks.

♙

Patient management

Register patient details, validate input and organize patient information for relevant workflows.

◷

Appointments

Support appointment workflows and a rule-based concept for emergency, chronic and routine priorities.

✚

Medical records

Structure patient, doctor, diagnosis and treatment information in related clinical workflows.

▤

Prescriptions

Organize medicine selection, dosage instructions and prescription-related checks.

▦

Medicine inventory

Support stock visibility and quantity checks connected to prescription workflows.

₹

Billing workflows

Billing and payment management are part of the project scope; specific capabilities depend on implemented modules.

Access by responsibility

Designed around user roles.

Role-based authorization is a core design goal. Available operations depend on the current implementation.
